# Code of conduct

Plane Thing is a small project for people who like aircraft and tinkering. We want it to be a pleasant place to ask questions,
report problems and share what you saw in the sky.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and assume good faith.** Most problems are misunderstandings, and many contributors are beginners.
- **Be specific and be honest.** Say what you saw, what you expected and what you tried. If you are not sure, say so.
- **Respect privacy.** Do not post other people's locations, home addresses, travel patterns or identifying details. That
  includes exports of a spotter log, which can show where someone drives. Aircraft registrations are public; the people
  watching them are not public property.
- **Do not harass, demean or threaten anyone.** That covers insults, discrimination, sexual language, repeated unwanted
  contact and publishing private information.
- **Keep it on topic.** Politics, promotion and spam do not belong in issues and pull requests.
- **Disagree about ideas, not people.** Direct criticism of code, designs and decisions is welcome. Personal attacks are not.

## Safety around aircraft and driving

Please do not use this project to discuss or plan interfering with aircraft, airports or air traffic, and do not encourage
anyone to look at a screen while driving.

## What happens when it is broken

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people from the repository when
they do not follow this code. They will try to explain, but do not have to.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior is a problem, report it privately using the form described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (give it a
title that starts with "Conduct report"), or report the content to GitHub. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept
private as far as possible.

## Scope

This applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone is clearly acting for the
project.
